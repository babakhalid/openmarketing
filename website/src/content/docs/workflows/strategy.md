`/strategy <slug>` turns customer/market evidence and the current diagnosis into a coherent direction before execution planning begins.

## What it produces

- Strategic foundation: best-fit segment, category, positioning, message, offer, proof, and primary conversion action.
- Relevant acquisition, activation, retention, referral, and revenue priorities.
- No more than three near-term bets tied to evidence, ownership, and learning objectives.
- Explicit channels and tactics that will not be pursued now.
- Prerequisites, signals, decision rules, risks, and human-approval gates.

Meaningful spend, a public launch, or a difficult-to-reverse choice triggers an adversarial `strategist` review before delivery.

## Output

`outputs/campaigns/<slug>-strategy.md`.
