`/psychology <slug>` explains what may help a specific customer understand, trust, start, and continue using the product. Recommendations connect evidence to messaging, offers, conversion, onboarding, lifecycle, or creative where relevant.

## Frameworks applied

- **Jobs To Be Done**: functional, emotional, and social progress.
- **Schwartz stages of awareness**: unaware → most aware.
- **Motivation**: autonomy, competence, belonging, status, safety, novelty, control, or relief when evidence supports it.
- **Friction map**: cognitive, effort, financial, social, trust, switching, and implementation costs.
- **Influence**: reciprocity, commitment/consistency, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity, and unity.
- **Proof and risk**: which claims need data, demos, peers, trials, guarantees, documentation, or transparent limitations.

## Output

`outputs/campaigns/<slug>-psychology.md` — journey state, jobs and motivations, friction and objections, proof needs, recommendations, surface-specific applications, evidence versus inference, and conflicts. Dark patterns and invented scarcity/proof are prohibited.
