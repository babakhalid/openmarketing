The `market-researcher` subagent gathers evidence on competitors, substitute and DIY alternatives, audiences, demand, category changes, distribution patterns, and constraints. Important findings retain a direct URL or named source artifact.

Tools granted: `read`, `write`, `edit`, `bash`, `grep`, `find`, `ls`, `web_search`, `fetch_content`, `get_search_content`.

Output schema: evidence table (numbered IDs), findings with inline `[N]` citations, numbered Sources, Coverage Status.
